Staff help

Sign in and out

Use your assigned username and password on the staff login page. When finished, select Logout in the navigation bar—especially on a shared computer.

Register and search appointments

First register the patient if needed. Open Appointments, select Register appointment, choose the patient, active dentist and treatment, then enter a future date and time. Search later using the unique appointment number.

Update, complete or cancel

Open an appointment to reschedule it. Use Mark completed after care is delivered, or Cancel when it will not proceed. A cancelled slot becomes available again.

Generate and print bills

Open Billing and enter an appointment number. The system copies the treatment charge, adds the configured consultation fee, saves one bill, and shows a receipt. Select Print receipt for the print-friendly version.

Reports

Open Reports, choose a date range, and optionally filter by dentist or status. The page shows matching appointments and revenue recorded for that period.
